### REBUILT Game Summary

In REBUILT, two alliances score **fuel** (game pieces) into alternating **hubs** and **climb a tower**:

- **Hubs** alternate being "active" — only the active hub scores points
- The alliance with more AUTO points gets their hub set to inactive first
- In the final period, all hubs become active
- **Fuel** can be pre-loaded, obtained from human player, collected at depot, or picked up from the field
- **Tower climbing**: 3 levels (L1=10pts teleop/15pts auto, L2=20pts, L3=30pts)
- **Ranking Points**: Energized (100+ fuel pts), Supercharged (360+ fuel pts), Traversal (50+ tower pts)
